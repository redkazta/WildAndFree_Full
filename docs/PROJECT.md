# Product Definition: Wild And Free

## What is it?

SaaS white-label platform for established music crews with existing fanbases.

## Target user

A music crew (collective, label, artist group) that:
- Already has an established fanbase
- Wants to sell merch and exclusive content
- Needs a unified community space
- Wants to own the fan relationship (not depend on Instagram, Spotify, etc.)

## Value proposition

Instead of scattering your presence across Linktree, Spotify, Shopify, Discord, Bandsintown, and Patreon, **Wild And Free gives you a single branded hub** that you control.

| For the Crew | For the Fans |
|-------------|---------------|
| Branded digital presence (white-label) | Personalized profiles |
| Sell merch and exclusive content | Interact with artists and other fans |
| Manage their community | Access exclusive content |
| Monetize directly | Socialize (friendship, messaging, wall) |
| ERP to manage orders | Own identity within the crew |

## Core principles

1. **Single-tenant by design**: one crew at a time, full focus.
2. **Role-based**: Admin → Artist → Fan/Member, each with distinct permissions.
3. **Community-first**: wall, messaging, friendship, not just static pages.
4. **Own the data**: the crew owns the fan relationship and the data.
5. **Mobile-ready**: artists and team manage from their phones.

## Roles

| Role | Description | Can do |
|------|-------------|--------|
| **Admin** | Crew management | Full access: ERP, manage artists, moderate content |
| **Artist** | Crew member | Update profile, post to wall, manage messages, upload music |
| **Fan/Member** | Registered user | Follow artists, interact with members, buy merch |

## Social rules

- **Wall**: Public. Each user has their own wall (like Metroflog).
- **Friendship**: Member↔Member: mutual request, unlocks messaging. Fan→Artist: follow only.
- **Messaging**: 1:1 and group. Artists have a message request inbox (not anyone can DM).
- **Profiles**: Artists can update from their app (statuses, music, info).

## Modules

```
1.  Auth & Roles
2.  Profiles (artist / fan)
3.  Wall (public feed per user)
4.  Friendship / Follow
5.  Messaging (1:1 + group + requests)
6.  Tags / VIP tiers
7.  Music / Spotify integration
8.  Podcasts
9.  Radio
10. Events
11. Shop (physical + digital merch)
12. ERP (orders, payments, deliveries, inventory)
13. Admin panel (web)
14. Mobile app (artist profile + team ERP)
```

## What it is NOT

- A general social network (it's crew-specific).
- A music streaming platform competing with Spotify.
- A standalone content creator tool.

It is a **white-label operating system for music collectives**.
